This kit starts from Australian rules. The Excel calculator uses the Cleaning Services Award 2020 rates that apply from 1 July 2026, copied from the Fair Work Ombudsman pay guide, plus 12% super and GST.

Enter your own numbers and it shows what one staff hour really costs. That includes the casual loading, the part-time allowance, weekend and shift penalty rates, leave and workers' comp. It then works out the cost of a typical job, a suggested price ex and inc GST, the hourly equivalent and how many jobs a month you need to break even. It works whether you clean on your own or employ cleaners.

The Quick quote sheet prices domestic and end-of-lease cleans by room count or hours, and offices by square metres and visits per week. It applies the award's minimum shift for part-time and casual staff.

The 13-page guide includes a fill-in quote template, three job checklists, a checklist of agreement clauses to think about, and ABN, GST, super, insurance and TPAR basics with official links. Every rate lists its source.
