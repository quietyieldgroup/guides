Opening your own physio, psychology, OT, podiatry, speech pathology, dietetics, chiro or osteo practice? Before you can bill Medicare, DVA or a health fund, there is a chain of registrations to get through, in the right order. Services Australia says most Medicare provider number applications take up to 28 days, and you need a number for every location.

This guide puts the whole chain in one place, step by step: ABN and GST, Ahpra or your professional body, PRODA and HPOS, your Medicare provider number, DVA, the major health funds, HICAPS, workers' compensation and NDIS. The steps say what to have ready, how long it takes where an official time is published, and where people get stuck, like the HPOS locality field that errors over 24 characters.

It also covers what to redo when you buy a practice, move or add a location, and how to make your practice manager an HPOS delegate.

Each rule is taken from an official page, or a fund's or HICAPS's own provider page, as of 17 November 2026. The links are included so you can check before you act, because rules change.
