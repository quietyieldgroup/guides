Want to keep your contractor payments ready for the TPAR?

The TPAR Contractor Payments Tracker (2026-27) is an Excel workbook plus an 8-page guide. You list each contractor once (name, ABN, address) and add a row for each payment: date paid, invoice, gross amount, GST, and any tax withheld because no ABN was quoted. The Year summary totals each contractor in whole dollars.

Built-in checks flag a missing address, an ABN that fails the check-digit test, a payment dated outside 2026-27, GST above one-eleventh of the gross, and a no-ABN payment with nothing withheld. A Check sheet walks through the same 10% and building and construction tests as this page. The guide covers who must lodge, what to record, which payments to leave out, how lodging works and mistakes to avoid.
