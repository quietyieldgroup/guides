Tenders feel like a closed shop because much of the work never goes public. Under the standard council rules, Queensland councils must invite written quotes from at least three suppliers for jobs from $21,900 to under $292,000, and they choose who to ask. If buyers can't find you, you're not asked.

This 2026 guide is written for builders, electricians, plumbers, landscapers, cleaners and civil firms with 1 to 20 staff. It shows where Queensland state, council, Main Roads, Health and university work is actually listed, and how to set up the main free official alerts. It explains council buying at each contract size, including councils that invite local firms first for jobs up to $500,000. It covers how to get onto Local Buy, council and QBuild panels, and how to use the Queensland Procurement Policy 2026's local and small business rules honestly.

Then it gives you the working pages: a documents checklist, a fill-in capability statement, a bid/no-bid scoring sheet and a first-30-days plan.

Facts come from official government and council sources, plus a few labelled exceptions such as our own count of the public tender feed. Current as of 17 November 2026. General information, not legal advice.
