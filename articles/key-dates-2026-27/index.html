Australian small business key dates 2026-27

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information, not advice.

This is one page of 2026-27 dates for Australian small businesses: allied health practices, trades, cleaning businesses, businesses that pay contractors, and the bookkeepers who look after them. Each date has who it applies to, one line on what to do, and a link to the official page it comes from. Where we worked a date out ourselves, for example moving a weekend due date to the Monday, it says "our count". The list is deliberately short: it only includes dates we have checked against official pages, not every date in the tax year.

Coming up next

The next six dates from 19 November 2026 (not counting the monthly BAS on the 21st):

  1. Mon 30 November 2026: Ahpra renewal deadline for OTs, physios, psychologists, podiatrists, chiropractors, osteopaths and the other non-medical, non-nursing professions.
  2. Tue 1 December 2026: Ahpra late period starts (our count). A late payment fee applies to renewals from now.
  3. Thu 10 December 2026: Privacy policy rules on automated decisions (APP 1.7 and 1.8) start.
  4. Thu 31 December 2026: Ahpra late period ends (our count). Unrenewed registration lapses.
  5. Fri 1 January 2027: Ahpra fast-track period opens for lapsed general or specialist registration (to 31 January).
  6. Mon 1 February 2027: Local Buy opens supplier tenders.

Weekend dates: the ATO's BAS page says that if a due date is on a weekend or public holiday, you have until the next business day. For the August 2027 TPAR, the Monday comes from the ATO's general due-date rule; no TPAR page states it. We haven't checked every state's public holidays against these dates.

Deadlines that run from your own dates

These don't fall on a fixed calendar day, so they aren't in the table:

  • Super: Must reach the employee's fund within 7 business days after each payday (longer in some situations). (ATO: Payday Super deadlines)
  • GST registration: Once your GST turnover reaches $75,000 or more, register within 21 days. If you're not registered, check each month. (ATO: Registering for GST)
  • ABN details: Update them within 28 days of becoming aware of a change. (ABR: Applying for an ABN)
  • HPOS delegations: Last 12 months at most. Delegation requests and renewals must be actioned within 14 days. (Services Australia: HPOS delegations)
  • New Medicare provider number for an extra location: Wait 2 business days before you submit claims with it. (Services Australia: Additional provider numbers)
  • NDIS worker screening: Valid for up to 5 years. You can apply to renew up to 90 days before it expires. (NDIS Commission: Worker screening)
  • NDIS registration: You can start re-registration any time in the 6 months before expiry. Start after expiry and it has lapsed. (NDIS Commission: Re-registration)

Which dates are yours

Allied health practices

Your big dates are Ahpra's: reminders from early October, renewal by 30 November, the December late period and January fast-track. Add NDIS re-registration and worker screening if you're a registered NDIS provider, the 10 December privacy policy change, and the payroll and BAS dates if you employ staff. Speech pathologists and dietitians aren't registered with Ahpra, so follow your professional association's dates (our note).

The Cleaning Services Award rates that started with the first full pay period on or after 1 July 2026 and 12% super set your labour cost for the year. If you pay subcontract cleaners, check whether you need to lodge a TPAR by August.

Businesses paying contractors

If you pay contractors for building and construction, cleaning, courier or road freight, IT, or security, investigation or surveillance services and the ATO's tests say you must lodge, 30 June 2027 closes the year's records and the 2026-27 TPAR is due by 28 August 2027 (Monday 30 August, our count, as the 28th is a Saturday).

For clients, the dates that repeat are the BAS dates (monthly on the 21st, quarterly on 28 July, 28 October, 28 February (Monday 1 March 2027, our count) and 28 April in this table), STP finalisation by 14 July, super within 7 business days of each payday, and the TPAR in August.
