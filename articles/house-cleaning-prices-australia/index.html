How Much to Charge for House Cleaning in Australia (2026)

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information only, not legal, tax, financial or employment advice. Award rates usually change every 1 July, so check the current Fair Work Ombudsman pay guide before you rely on any rate.

There is no official price for a house clean in Australia, and this page doesn't quote what other cleaners charge. The honest answer to "how much should I charge?" is: enough to cover the hours the clean really takes, what each hour costs you, the costs of every visit, and your profit, plus GST if you're registered.

Charge per job, worked out from hours

Time is most of your cost, so start from hours. But every visit also carries costs that don't shrink when the clean is short: travel, fuel, supplies and overheads. So quote a regular clean as a price per visit, built up in this order:

  1. Hours on site. Time each room type in your own jobs and add them up.
  2. Labour cost per hour. For employees, the award rate plus a leave on-cost for full-time and part-time staff, plus super. If you clean yourself, the hourly amount you want to earn; nobody pays your super or leave, so build them in.
  3. Per-visit costs: paid travel time, supplies and vehicle cost per kilometre.
  4. Overheads per cleaning hour: insurance, software, and your time quoting and invoicing.
  5. Margin. Divide cost by (1 − margin). A 15% margin means $15 profit in every $100 of price (ex GST).
  6. GST, if you are registered. One-eleventh of what the customer pays is GST you pass on (business.gov.au).

The hourly rate is then a result, not a starting point: price ex GST ÷ hours on site.

Worked example: a 3-bedroom regular clean

These are the example values in the paid kit's spreadsheet, not a market price. The house: 3 bedrooms at 15 minutes each, 2 bathrooms at 30, a separate toilet at 10, the kitchen at 30, 2 living areas at 20 and the laundry at 10. That is 195 minutes, or 3.25 hours. The minutes per room are example timings, not industry standards.

The cleaner is a casual Level 1 with 10% of hours on Saturdays under the Cleaning Services Award 2020 [MA000022], which costs $39.43 an hour including 12% super, the 2026-27 rate per the ATO. Also: 30 minutes' paid travel, $8 supplies, 20 km at $1.01/km, overheads of $1,100 a month over 40 jobs of 3 hours ($9.17 per cleaning hour), a 15% margin and GST registration. Why short cleans cost more per hour

Run the same inputs for a 2-hour clean and the price is $170.71 ex GST, about $85.36 per cleaning hour (our arithmetic, using the kit's formulas). The $47.91 of travel, supplies and vehicle doesn't shrink with the job, so one hourly rate for every house loses money on small homes.

Staff add a floor. Under clause 13.5 of the award, part-time and casual cleaners must be paid a minimum shift "even if the employee works for a shorter time": 2 hours at a location up to 2,000 m², or 1 hour only for a lone cleaner at a stand-alone location of 300 m² or less where a longer shift across locations isn't practicable. Price the paid hours.

Agree the scope, then the rules

Price extras such as inside the oven, interior windows and ironing separately.

  • Award coverage. The award covers "the business of providing cleaning services under a contract" (clause 4.2). If you aren't sure it covers your business or staff, check with Fair Work. Its contract cleaning page notes cleaners may work for a private household, and that "Independent contractors are not covered by awards."
  • Consumer guarantees. The ACCC says consumers automatically get guarantees when they buy services, including due care and skill. Don't write terms that try to remove them.
  • GST. You must register once your GST turnover reaches $75,000, within 21 days (ATO). If you aren't registered, don't include GST, and show on invoices that GST was not included.
