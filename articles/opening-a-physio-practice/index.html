Opening a Physio Practice in Australia: 2026 Setup Checklist

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules, forms and processing times change, so check each linked official page (Ahpra, Services Australia, the ATO, DVA, the funds and your state workers' compensation body) before you apply.

Leaving a clinic to open your own physiotherapy practice? Before your first patient can claim, there is a chain of registrations to get through. Here are the questions physios ask, answered from the official pages.

Is my registration ready?

You are registered through the Physiotherapy Board of Australia (Ahpra). Ahpra says other health practitioners, which covers physiotherapists, "renew their registration by 30 November every year". This year that is Monday 30 November 2026 (provisional or limited registration renews on the anniversary it was granted instead). Renew in December's one-month late period and you pay a late fee on top; miss that too and your registration lapses and your name is removed from the register (Ahpra: Registration renewal). Do it before anything else here: Services Australia says you're not eligible to claim Medicare benefits if your registration has been suspended or has expired.

Your professional indemnity insurance must meet your Board's standard "for all aspects of their practice": confirm it covers private practice.

What order do the registrations go in?

  1. Ahpra registration current.
  2. ABN, plus a business name if you won't trade under your own name, and GST if it applies.
  3. PRODA, your individual government login.
  4. HPOS, linked in PRODA, where you apply for provider numbers.
  5. A Medicare provider number for each practice location.
  6. Then DVA, the health funds, HICAPS and workers' compensation.

How long does the Medicare provider number take?

Physiotherapists are on Services Australia's list of allied health professionals who may qualify for Medicare (Services Australia: Eligibility). The points that set your opening date:

  • "Most Medicare provider number applications take up to 28 days to process", and some take longer.
  • There is a surge in applications between December and April, processed in order of receipt.
  • You need a unique number for each place of practice, and "You can't claim a Medicare benefit until we've issued your provider number for that location."
  • Submit before your proposed location start date, with all supporting documents.

Home visits and a home address

Services Australia can't suppress a provider number address, so a home address used as a practice location may be publicly available. There is a narrow exception: if your home is your only practice location and you only provide telehealth or mobile consultations, an alternative address such as a PO Box registered to you may be recorded, on a signed request.

Is physiotherapy GST-free?

Physiotherapy is on the ATO's list of GST-free health services, as long as the service is performed by a recognised professional and is generally accepted in the profession as necessary for the patient's treatment (ATO: GST and other health services). Massage alone is not GST-free, but it can be when supplied as part of physiotherapy by a physio trained in massage, where the profession treats it as a standard part of treatment. Ask your accountant whether to register for GST.

Will DVA and the funds pick me up automatically?

Mostly. DVA registers physiotherapists automatically when they get a Medicare provider number, except lymphoedema, pelvic health and vestibular physiotherapy providers, who register directly. Form D9537 (ABN and GST status) is a separate step for each payee provider number.

Workers' compensation patients

  • Queensland: WorkCover Queensland's approved registered providers include physiotherapists.
  • New South Wales: physiotherapists need SIRA approval to be treated as appropriately qualified to provide services in the workers compensation system.
  • Victoria: complete WorkSafe's registration form with your Physiotherapy Board of Australia registration number and keep at least $1 million of professional indemnity cover at all times. If you are registered with Medicare, your Medicare number(s) are used as your WorkSafe provider number (WorkSafe Victoria: physiotherapy).

Before your website goes live

Ahpra's advertising rules ban testimonials about clinical aspects (symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, outcome), including on your clinic Facebook page. Practitioners without specialist registration should be wary of phrases such as "specialises in" (Ahpra: advertising requirements).
