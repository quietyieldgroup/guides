Cleaning Award Weekend and Public Holiday Rates 2026-27

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information only, not legal, tax, financial or employment advice. Award rates usually change every 1 July, so check the current Fair Work Ombudsman pay guide before you rely on any rate.

Under the Cleaning Services Award, a full-time Level 1 cleaner earns $40.62 an hour on a Saturday, $54.16 on a Sunday and $67.70 on a public holiday. Part-time and casual rates are higher again. Here are all the rates for 2026-27, the percentages they come from, and what they do to the cost of a weekend job.

Where the rates come from

Award Table 7 (clause 20.2) sets each penalty as a percentage of the minimum hourly rate:

Hours workedFull-timePart-timeCasual
Midnight Friday to midnight Saturday150%165%175%
Midnight Saturday to midnight Sunday200%215%225%
Public holiday250%265%275%

The part-time and casual columns already include the 15% part-time allowance and the 25% casual loading: the award's headings say "inclusive of part-time allowance" and "inclusive of casual loading". So you don't add the loading on top. A casual Level 1 on a Saturday is 175% of $27.08, which is $47.39. We checked every Saturday, Sunday and public holiday figure in the table above against these percentages, and all 27 match.

"Saturday" means all hours from midnight Friday to midnight Saturday, so a 5am to 11am Saturday shift is all at the Saturday rate. Which days are public holidays depends on your state or territory: Fair Work says "Public holidays can be different depending on the state or territory you work in" (Fair Work).

Overtime on weekends and public holidays

Overtime has its own rates (Table 5). For full-time and part-time staff, Monday to Saturday overtime is 150% for the first 2 hours and 200% after that; Sunday is 200% all day and public holidays 250%. For casuals the same rows are 175%, 225%, 225% and 275%. For part-time and casual staff, "All time worked in excess of 7.6 hours per day, five days per week or 38 hours in any week" is overtime (clause 19.2).

What weekend work does to your cost

A price that only covers the weekday rate can't pay the award. Fair Work found that the most common breaches in its investigation of 17 Adelaide commercial cleaning companies were "failures to pay penalty rates, underpayments of minimum hourly rates of pay and failure to make and keep employee records" (media release, 13 January 2022). Those businesses were chosen because of concerns raised, so it isn't a random sample.

To price a roster, blend the rates by the share of hours on each day, then add 12% super for 2026-27 (ATO). For a casual Level 1:

The 10% row is the kit's worked example; the others are our arithmetic. Two more things to price in:

  • Leave loading. When full-time and part-time staff take annual leave, the award pays the greater of 17.5% of the minimum hourly rate or the shift, weekend or public holiday penalties they would have received (clause 21.3). Weekend-rostered staff cost more on leave too.
  • Minimum shifts. A part-time or casual cleaner must be paid at least 2 hours at a site of up to 2,000 m² (clause 13.5), so a short Sunday job is paid at the Sunday rate for the full minimum.
