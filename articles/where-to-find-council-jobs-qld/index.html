Where Are Queensland Council Tenders Listed? 2026 Guide

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Buyers change platforms and pages move, so check the linked pages and each council's procurement page.

Queensland state agencies and councils now share one tendering platform, VendorPanel, which you reach through supply.qld.gov.au. QTenders is the state's tender directory. Brisbane City Council uses its own SAP Ariba portal, and councils also post on their own websites and social media. But much smaller council work is invited quotes that usually aren't published.

VendorPanel: the shared state and council platform

Queensland Health says "The Queensland Government has standardised procurement across all agencies through the adoption of VendorPanel as its single procurement solution, which replaced the QTenders system." The supply portal calls it a joint venture "to create a common procurement platform". Buyers on it include Queensland and local governments, government owned corporations, not-for-profits and universities.

  • It is "a single supplier portal featuring Queensland and local government tender opportunities".
  • Supplier profiles are free, and there are no fees to quote, tender or supply.
  • Once registered, "You will start to receive notifications of public tenders for the supply categories and regions of service you selected during registration." The portal advises registering "into all supply categories (Marketplace Lists) that match your business capability".
  • Its Public Tenders page lists tenders with filters and an RSS feed. VendorPanel's terms limit use to your own internal business purposes, so don't republish what you find.

QTenders: the state's directory

QTenders is the Queensland Government Tenders Directory. Under the procurement policy, when agencies use an open offer they "must use the Queensland Procurement Solution to publish the tender details, this will then be displayed on the Queensland Government QTenders website". Queensland Health says QTenders is now used "to access information about upcoming, current, and closed procurement opportunities only": to respond to Health, you must register on VendorPanel.

Council websites, Brisbane and social media

Under the default rules in the Local Government Regulation, a council's large contracts ($292,000 and over, ex GST, after the 1 July 2026 indexation per one council's policy) go to tender and are published on the council's website for at least 21 days. Councils also advertise elsewhere: Balonne on its website, Facebook page and VendorPanel's mobile app; Southern Downs on its website, Facebook and LinkedIn. Sunshine Coast runs a tender alert service that emails you "when new public tenders are advertised".

Brisbane City Council, the largest council, isn't on VendorPanel. "All public Council tenders are published in our supplier portal, SAP Ariba, and on our current tenders page." Registration has 2 steps (allow 2 business days for approval) and no fees with a standard SAP Business Network account. Alerts come by the UNSPSC category you choose.

A snapshot from the guide's researchers, labelled as their own count: on 16-17 November 2026 the public feed held 290 open tenders from 88 Queensland issuers, and 39 of 77 councils had an open item on 17 November.

Work that's coming up

The Forward Procurement Pipeline is "An interactive dashboard which provides information on future procurement opportunities prior to a tender being released". Agencies can give details up to ten years ahead. Its open data was last updated on 16 November 2026, on a non-regular schedule. Moreton Bay publishes a contracting plan of planned significant procurement for the financial year.

The work you won't find listed

Under the default rules, medium council jobs ($21,900 to under $292,000) need written quotes from at least 3 suppliers the council chooses, and many councils pick them from VendorPanel Marketplace categories. Local Buy arrangement quotes go only to its pre-qualified suppliers. Those requests usually aren't published, so being registered in the right categories is how you get asked.
