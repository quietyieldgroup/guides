How to Get on a Council Supplier Panel in Queensland

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Panel rules, fees and opening dates change, so check the linked pages and each council's procurement page before you apply.

In Queensland you get onto a council supplier panel by answering a tender: a Local Buy tender, for arrangements many councils buy through, or a council's own tender when it opens or refreshes its panel. State building work has a separate free register. Here is how each works.

What a "panel" is

Government buyers use several kinds of list. The Queensland Procurement Policy 2026 groups them as common-use supply arrangements, "including without limitation a standing offer arrangement, a register of pre-qualified suppliers, panel arrangement or preferred supplier arrangement". Councils use the same idea. Under the Local Government Regulation's default rules, a council can buy from an approved contractor list, a register of pre-qualified suppliers, a preferred supplier arrangement or an LGA arrangement instead of seeking quotes or tenders. Moreton Bay, for example, lists buying "under an existing Council supplier panel arrangement, Local Buy arrangement, or other government agency arrangement".

Local Buy: the panels many councils share

Local Buy is "a profit-for-purpose organisation owned 100% by the LGAQ". Its home page says 71 Queensland councils use its procurement platform. It runs more than 50 arrangements across 7 categories; construction examples include Building & Construction - Residential (LB331), Road and General Civil Construction (LB313) and Traffic Management Services (LB325).

How you join

  1. Find the arrangement that matches your work. "To become a Local Buy Supplier, your business must respond to the relevant tender/s" (Local Buy: Become a supplier).
  2. Watch the windows. Local Buy opens tenders on 1 February, 1 May, 1 August and 1 November each year. "Generally, tenders are open for the entire month", so the 1 November 2026 round may still be open as you read this: check Local Buy's open tenders. If the pattern holds, the next window opens 1 February 2027.
  3. Respond through APET360, Local Buy's online tendering portal. "The tender process takes three months."
  4. Have the insurance. "The mandatory minimum level of insurance cover for all Local Buy Suppliers is $20M Public Liability Insurance."

Local Buy holds monthly Supplier Welcome Sessions, in Brisbane and online. Once you're on, quotes under an arrangement go privately to pre-qualified suppliers through VendorPanel, not to the public feed.

What it costs

Local Buy's fees page lists an initial appointment fee of $500 and an annual renewal of $250 (ex GST), plus a Contract Management Fee that varies by arrangement, from 0.75% to 2%. Local Buy waives the Supplier Verification Fee (not the Contract Management Fee) for certain businesses: for example, aggregated revenue under $500,000 a year, or sole traders, with a registered head office in Queensland, Tasmania or the Northern Territory. Proof of eligibility is required.

A council's own panel

Some councils keep their own preferred or pre-qualified supplier lists. Southern Downs is a sourced example: to join, suppliers "must go through the official tender process", and lists "can be refreshed approximately once a year". It also warns that "acceptance on these lists does not guarantee any amount of business".

  • Ask each council you can service when its trade panels next open.
  • Register on VendorPanel, through supply.qld.gov.au, in every category that fits your work, so you're notified of public tenders in those categories. Some councils also pick who to invite to quote from them: Southern Downs selects at least 3 suppliers in the relevant category.
  • Go to supplier briefings where councils run them. Moreton Bay hosts supplier briefing sessions.

State building work: QBuild and PQC

  • QBuild keeps a register of local contractors and suppliers for maintenance and minor works, generally projects under $1 million. "Registration is free" and stays active for 2 years. You must provide evidence of public liability insurance, and personal injury insurance if you're self-employed. You don't need WorkCover if you have no employees. For low-value work, QBuild usually requests quotes from a shortlist from the register. "Being registered doesn't guarantee you'll get work with QBuild."
  • The PQC System is the state's register of prequalified building consultants and contractors. Contractors must be prequalified for projects over $1 million. There is no fee to apply; you need a Digital ID. Contractors undergo a financial assessment before being awarded a contract.

Being on a panel isn't the same as getting work

A Commonwealth small business inquiry, as reported in the trade press, heard that many small businesses "have been on panels for years and never been approached for a request to quote". Once you're on, keep your profile complete and quote well on the small jobs. To see which council buying rule applies to a job's value, try the free Qld council quote and tender threshold check.
