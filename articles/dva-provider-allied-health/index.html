DVA Provider Registration for Allied Health: 2026 Guide

Current as of 19 November 2026. General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice. DVA and Services Australia pages change, so check the linked pages before you treat your first DVA client.

For most allied health practitioners there is no separate DVA application. DVA says "Most healthcare providers are automatically registered with us when they receive their Medicare provider number." What you do need is that Medicare number, the right tax paperwork if you're registered for GST, and to accept DVA's fees. A few provider types must register with DVA directly. Here is who, and what to send.

Step 1: get your Medicare provider number

"To become a DVA service provider you will need to have a Medicare provider number" (DVA: Become a DVA health care provider). From Services Australia:

  • You need a provider number for each practice location, and you can't claim a Medicare benefit until the number for that location is issued.
  • Most applications take up to 28 days, some longer. Services Australia says it sees a surge in applications between December and April.
  • Applying online through HPOS needs a PRODA account.
  • You're not eligible to claim Medicare benefits if your registration has been suspended or has expired.

Step 2: check whether you're registered automatically

DVA's automatic list includes:

  • audiologists, chiropractors, clinical psychologists, diabetes educators and dietitians
  • occupational therapists (general or mental health), optometrists or orthoptists, osteopaths and physiotherapists
  • podiatrists, psychologists, social workers (mental health) and speech pathologists.

It also lists dental providers. If you're on the list, your DVA registration follows from your Medicare provider number.

Provider types that register directly

DVA says "Some provider types need to register directly with DVA". Its list:

  • lymphoedema providers (physiotherapists and occupational therapists only)
  • neuropsychologists
  • optical dispensers and orthotists
  • pelvic health physiotherapy providers and vestibular physiotherapy providers
  • social workers (general).

If that's you, contact DVA's provider support team, using the email address on DVA's page, and include your provider type in your enquiry.

Step 3: the tax paperwork

D9537: Notification of Australian Business Number and reference identification for tax treatment purposes and Recipient Created Tax Invoice agreementOne for each payee provider numberSo DVA records your ABN and GST registration status. Claims can't be paid "inclusive of GST" on GST-applicable fee schedule items unless the form has been processed before your claim is assessed.
RCTI agreement (recipient-created tax invoice)"All providers who are required to pay GST"DVA pays you through Medicare, and to do this you need to agree to its RCTI process.

Someone you authorise in your organisation signs the RCTI agreement, and each entity that pays GST and provides services to DVA clients should sign a separate one. DVA's page gives post, email and fax options for sending it.

Am I registered for GST?

You need an ABN before you can register for GST. You must register once your GST turnover reaches $75,000, or when you start a new business and expect to reach that in the first year (ATO). Chiropractic, dietary, occupational therapy, osteopathy, physiotherapy, podiatry, psychology and speech pathology are on the ATO's list of GST-free health services, when the ATO's conditions are met. Below the threshold registration is optional, but if you choose to register you generally must stay registered for at least 12 months. So a practice can be registered for GST even if most of its services are GST-free, and DVA's D9537 records your status either way. Ask your accountant which applies to you.

Step 4: accept DVA's fees and conditions

As a DVA provider, DVA says "you agree to accept our fees and conditions as listed in the Notes for providers Fee schedule. No co-payments should be charged to our clients." Read the fee schedule before your first DVA appointment so your front desk knows not to ask for a gap.

Step 5: claim, and let veterans find you

  • HPOS offers DVA Webclaim, and a delegate you nominate in HPOS can be given access to DVA patient treatment reports.
  • DVA says you can show that you accept DVA Veteran Cards through the National Health Services Directory, which powers healthdirect's Service Finder. "Indicating you accept Veteran Cards does not create any new obligations."

Buying, moving or adding a location

You can't use anyone else's Medicare provider number, including a seller's. A new location means a new provider number, and DVA's D9537 is sent for each payee provider number, so send one for each new number. To work out how early to start, try the free allied health registration timeline.
